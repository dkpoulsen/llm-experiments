- [[deepspeed-ulysses-system-optimizations-for-enabling]] — DEEPSPEED ULYSSES: SYSTEM OPTIMIZATIONS FOR ENABLING — DeepSpeed Ulysses (arXiv 2309.14509). Sequence parallelism via all-to-all on the head dimension: 'Communication reduction of over 10x compared to existing systems, resulting in throughput improvements of up to 2.5x, and sustained throughput of over 175 TFlops/GPU (over 54% of hardware peak)'. Measured on up to 256 A100s with 7B and 30B GPT models. Table 3 (weak scaling, GPT-7B dense, global batch 8): 65,536 tokens on 64 GPUs = 9,676.76 ms at 161.36 TFLOPs/GPU; 131,072 on 128 GPUs = 17,052.5 ms at 157.41 TFLOPs/GPU; 262,144 on 256 GPUs = 33,486.5 ms at 147.4 TFLOPs/GPU. Per-GPU TFLOPs is therefore nearly FLAT (161->157->147, -9%) as sequence length doubles twice, i.e. the quadratic attention FLOPs convert to wall-clock at roughly constant efficiency for a 7B model. Also reports a 1.3B GPT convergence run at 32K on 8 A100s with SP degree 4. `review`
- [[loongtrain-efficient-training-of-long-sequence-llms]] — LoongTrain: Efficient Training of Long-Sequence LLMs — LoongTrain (arXiv 2406.18485). Head-context parallelism (2D-attention) for long-sequence training; improves MFU by up to 2.88x vs Megatron-CP and 1.49-1.53x vs DS-Ulysses. End-to-end MFU of 7B-MHA and 7B-GQA on 64 GPUs (Table 3) ranges 0.360-0.518 across 128K-1M sequence lengths; Ring-Attention's MFU is 'less than 10%' at 128K for MHA and reaches 19.6% (GQA) / 40.6% at 1M. So at 128K-1M on 64 A100s, long-context MFU for the best system is 36-52% - comparable to short-context MFU - which again implies the quadratic FLOPs penalty is paid in wall-clock rather than amortized away. No 4k baseline is reported. `review`
- [[ring-attention-with-blockwise]] — Ring Attention with Blockwise — Ring Attention (arXiv 2310.01889). Blockwise transformer ring-passing over K/V blocks across devices, enabling context length to scale linearly with device count (7B at 256k on 8xA100, 30B at 2048k on TPUv4-1024, 65B at 1024k). Evaluates MFU across model size and context length with FSDP/TP and 2M-token batches on 8/32xA100 and 4M on TPUv4-256, arguing MFU is maintained as context grows because communication is overlapped with blockwise computation.
