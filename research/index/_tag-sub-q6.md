It combines pipeline parallelism with fully sharded data parallelism and schedules micro-batches breadth-first so that FSDP unsharding is amortized across many micro-batches instead of being repeated per micro-batch. Reported gain: up to 43% higher training throughput for a 52B-parameter model at a small batch size per GPU compared to Megatron-LM, with lower memory usage. - [[megatron-lm-training-multi-billion-parameter-language-models-using]] — Megatron-LM: Training Multi-Billion Parameter Language Models Using — Shoeybi et al. (NVIDIA), the canonical tensor-parallelism paper and the primary source for TP's communication bill. A transformer layer under Megatron TP needs exactly 2 all-reduces in the forward pass and 2 in the backward pass (4 communication ops per layer), implemented with conjugate f/g autograd operators; the MLP block alone costs 1 all-reduce forward + 1 backward, and the vocabulary-parallel output embedding adds an all-reduce plus an all-gather. Trains an 8.3B GPT-2-like model on 512 V100-32GB GPUs (32 DGX-2H servers) at 15.1 PFLOPs sustained and 76% scaling efficiency against a single-GPU baseline of 39 TFLOPS (30% of peak). Infrastructure: 300 GB/s intra-server NVSwitch, 100 GB/s inter-server over 8 InfiniBand adapters. An 8B model requires 8-way model parallelism. This is the arithmetic behind 'TP is ruled out by the fabric': four all-reduces per layer, each blocking the next GEMM, on a link that is orders of magnitude slower than NVSwitch. `review`
- [[on-optimizing-the-communication-of-model-parallelism]] — ON OPTIMIZING THE COMMUNICATION OF MODEL PARALLELISM — Zhuang et al. (CMU / Tsinghua / Berkeley / MBZUAI): formalizes 'cross-mesh resharding', the many-to-many multicast that appears whenever intra-operator parallelism (TP) and inter-operator parallelism (PP) are combined - a sharded tensor must move from a source device mesh to a destination mesh and may change layout. Existing all-gather or P2P implementations are either sub-optimal or hard-coded to one parallelism plan; the paper proposes a broadcast-based communication system plus an overlap-friendly pipeline schedule. Microbenchmarks: up to 10x over existing approaches across tensor and mesh layouts; end-to-end multi-node training gains of 10% (GPT) and 50% (U-Transformer). Stated cluster assumptions: fast intra-node NVLink vs slow inter-node Ethernet/InfiniBand, fully-connected node topology with uniform pairwise bandwidth, host NIC as the bottleneck, full-duplex send/receive. Testbed is AWS p3.8xlarge nodes (4x V100-16GB) with 10 Gbps cross-node bandwidth. Relevant here because it isolates how much model-parallel cost is resharding traffic - exactly the traffic a PCIe-only, no-NVLink, no-P2P fabric makes dominant. `review`
- [[zeropp-unleashing-exceptional-parallelism-efficiency]] — ZeroPP: Unleashing Exceptional Parallelism Efficiency — ZeroPP (Shanghai AI Lab / Tsinghua): a TP-free framework that combines pipeline parallelism with ZeRO-3/FSDP, adding a ZeRO-compatible breadth-first PP schedule, decoupled gradient computation for a near-zero pipeline bubble, and recomputation. Theory: TP costs 8Bbsh communication volume per transformer block (four all-reduces across forward and backward); ZeroPP costs 36Bh^2/U per iteration (two all-gathers plus one reduce-scatter per scheduling unit, each three times the ~12h^2 parameter size), so ZeroPP is cheaper whenever 2sUb > 9h - a condition easily met at long sequence lengths and large batches. Microbenchmark on 8 GPUs with a 6.2B GPT: ZeRO-3 communication volume and iteration time beat TP at every global batch size, with the gap widening as batch grows. End-to-end: up to 64 A800-80GB GPUs across 8 InfiniBand nodes; up to 33% faster than Megatron 3D parallelism at comparable memory; a 14.6B model on 32 GPUs with PP=4 and global batch 192 shows the advantage growing as the memory limit tightens. Directly supports 'drop TP, use PP+FSDP' on a low-bandwidth fabric. `review`
