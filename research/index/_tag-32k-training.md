- [[hysparse2-hybrid-sparse-attention]] — HySparse2: Hybrid Sparse Attention — HySparse2 (Wei, Gao, Zhang et al., Xiaomi LLM-Core, arXiv 2609.26368) is a hybrid sparse attention architecture with two-level KV sharing, evaluated on an 80B-A3B MoE. Outer level: KV Bridging adopts a YOCO-style self-decoder/cross-decoder structure but bridges only full-attention layers — the KV caches for cross-decoder full-attention layers are generated from the hidden states of self-decoder full-attention layers, so prefill can exit after the self-decoder and skip all cross-decoder layers. Inner level: KV Reuse (HySparse's core design) with two refinements — block-level sparsity replaced by token-level sparsity for finer long-context retrieval, and the separate SWA branch removed in favour of forcing a sliding window of recent tokens into the sparse selection. Selection budget: 128 forced local tokens + 1,024 global tokens.

Training recipe (directly relevant as a 32k precedent): the 80B-A3B models are pretrained on approximately 500B tokens at a context length of 32k, then a light post-training stage adds ~100B tokens, introduces agentic data, and extends context to 256k. All three compared models share the same data mixture and schedule; Muon optimizer with a WSD schedule and peak learning rates of 1e-3 (pretraining) / 5e-5 (post-training). After pretraining HySparse2 retains broadly comparable general capabilities and improves long-context retrieval; after light post-training it improves mean MRCR-style retrieval and agentic multi-turn performance.

Cost results: at 1M tokens HySparse2 reduces prefill FLOPs by 2.92x relative to HySparse and 5.02x relative to Hybrid SWA, with KV cache of only 2.69 GB versus 6.72 GB (HySparse) and 12.09 GB (Hybrid SWA) under FP8 KV storage. Ablations show token-level selection beats block-level. 6,151 words. `review`
- [[native-sparse-attention-hardware-aligned-and-natively]] — Native Sparse Attention: Hardware-Aligned and Natively — NSA — Native Sparse Attention: Hardware-Aligned and Natively Trainable Sparse Attention (Yuan, Gao, Dai et al., DeepSeek-AI, arXiv 2502.11089). The canonical "natively trainable sparse attention" paper and the most directly relevant counterweight to the repo's CSA2-at-32k pessimism. NSA combines three branches — compressed attention, selected block attention, and a sliding window — with blockwise selection as the core hardware-alignment principle: key/value sequences are divided into selection blocks, block importance is scored, and top-n blocks are selected. The paper's argument is that token-granular selection (e.g. HashAttention) forces loading many individual KV tokens, which breaks the contiguous memory access that FlashAttention-style kernels depend on and degrades training efficiency; blockwise selection instead keeps contiguous access and enables full Tensor Core utilization.

Measured kernel results (Triton NSA vs Triton FlashAttention-2, same backend, A100): forward speedup 2.1x at 8k, 3.8x at 16k, 6.3x at 32k, 9.0x at 64k; backward speedup 1.1x at 8k, 2.0x at 16k, 3.4x at 32k, 6.0x at 64k; decode up to 11.6x at 64k. This is the key data point that sparse attention is ALREADY a net training win at 32k when selection is block-granular — which reconciles with LongCat's 0.83x net loss at 32K, measured on token-granular gather.

Training regime: a 27B-parameter backbone trained on 260B tokens; both the full-attention baseline and NSA are pretrained on 270B tokens of 8k-length texts, then continued-trained and SFT'd on 32k-length texts with YaRN. So NSA's own training context tops out at 32k — a direct precedent for a 32k sparse-attention experiment. Selection config: compression block 32, sliding stride 16, selected block size 64, 16 selected blocks (including 1 initial and 2 local blocks), sliding window 512, i.e. roughly 1,024 selected tokens plus a 512-token window at any context length. NSA matches or beats full attention on MMLU/MMLU-PRO/CMMLU/BBH/GSM8K/MATH/DROP/MBPP/HumanEval with a stable pretraining loss curve. Caveat: the speedups are against a Triton FlashAttention-2 baseline, not a tuned production kernel. 9,311 words. `review`
